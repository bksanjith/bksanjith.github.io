About

I am an Engineering Product Development student at SUTD, graduating in 2029. I learn by building and prefer to start from a real problem. My projects are on the Projects page, grouped into professional work, projects that develop my skills and projects built out of personal interest.

Together with my family, I have developed Fragrance Castle Singapore, a fragrance retail and wholesale business. I have won new wholesale clients, sold to both retail and wholesale customers, managed inventory and packed wholesale orders. It made me careful with money. Before I spend on anything, I want to know what it costs and when it will pay for itself. I am still learning, and I am now applying my engineering skills to real business solutions, starting with reducing the time it takes to print shipping labels.

During National Service I trained over 160 police officers from very different ages, backgrounds and outlooks. The aim was to push each of them hard enough that they would remember the experience, while looking after their welfare. Finding where that line sits for each person shaped how I work with people.

I see myself as a global individual and do not think within the limits of one country. I am building a network of people across Asia, the Middle East and Europe, partly through cross-industry events, including Beautyworld in Dubai every year and CeMAT Asia and PTC Asia in Shanghai. Interning in China with dsm-firmenich placed me in an environment very different from Singapore, in how people behave and how they work, and in a more technologically developed city. It taught me to adapt quickly and to be at ease working with people wherever they are and however they work.

I enjoy trying new things and meeting people who do them well, and I am usually keen to learn more when an interesting opportunity arises.

01 / ABOUT

One foot in engineering, the other in operations.

I study Engineering Product Development at SUTD, graduating in 2029. SUTD teaches through projects, so a large share of my grade comes from designing, building and testing things in teams rather than from exams alone. So far that has meant a solar food dryer with closed-loop humidity control, a variable capacitor tested to ± 2 nF, a rubber band car and a holographic notification device.

I've helped run Fragrance Castle Singapore, our family's fragrance retail and wholesale business, since 2019. I manage a 200+ SKU catalogue across six brands, set up our Shopee, Lazada and Shopify stores, and grew our wholesaler base by 60%. Running a small business taught me to ask what something costs and when it pays back before asking how clever it is. It is also where most of my side projects start, including SwiftPack.

Before university I served National Service as a Field Instructor (Sergeant 2) at the Singapore Police Force Training Command. I trained over 160 officers, including 79 Senior Officer Trainees I took through their nine-month course on my own. My trainees came from very different ages, backgrounds and outlooks, and learning to read and adapt to each of them is something I now bring into design work, where the user rarely thinks the way you do.

Outside class and work I design small things for home on my Bambu Lab X1C, and spend time at industry shows looking for ideas I can bring back. I'm joining Shopee SPX Express as a business development intern in January 2027, and I'm open to internships from September 2027.

Engineering with the business in view

I study Engineering Product Development at SUTD. Outside class I help run my family's fragrance business, and before university I trained police officers. Each of those changed how I build things.

SUTD teaches through projects, so a large share of my grade comes from designing, building and testing things in teams rather than from exams alone. That has meant a solar food dryer with humidity-controlled ventilation, a variable capacitor measured to ± 2 nF, a rubber band car and a holographic notification device.

I've helped run Fragrance Castle Singapore since 2019. Running a small business taught me to ask what something costs and when it pays back before asking how clever it is, and it's where most of my side projects start.

During National Service I trained over 160 officers at the Singapore Police Force Training Command. They came from very different ages, backgrounds and outlooks, and learning to read and adapt to each of them is something I now bring into design work, where the user rarely thinks the way you do.

I'm joining Shopee SPX Express as a business development intern in January 2027, and I'm open to internships from September 2027.

About me

I'm an Engineering Product Development student at SUTD, graduating in 2029. SUTD teaches through projects, so a large share of my grade comes from designing, building and testing things in teams rather than from exams alone.

I've helped run Fragrance Castle Singapore, my family's fragrance retail and wholesale business, since 2019. It taught me to ask what something costs and when it pays back before asking how clever it is.

During National Service I trained over 160 police officers. They came from very different ages, backgrounds and outlooks, and learning to read and adapt to each of them shaped how I work with people.

People tend to describe me as steady. I'd rather do a few things properly than many things halfway, and I like being the person who gets something sorted.

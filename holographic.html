Holographic email notifier

An ambient clock that shows priority email alerts as a floating image, using the Pepper's Ghost effect.

Role
Team member
Hardware
Raspberry Pi 4
Optics
Pepper's Ghost, 35°
Software
Python

The problem

At home you either keep your phone close and get pulled into every notification, or put it away and risk missing something urgent. We wanted a way to surface only the emails that matter, without a screen demanding attention.

The approach

We used the Pepper's Ghost effect, an old stage illusion where an angled transparent surface reflects a screen below it so the image seems to float. Most of the time the device shows a clock. When a priority email arrives, an alert appears in the same floating display.

  • Display: a small screen facing up into a transparent surface set at 35°
  • Compute: Raspberry Pi 4 (4 GB) running Python
  • Email: filtered so only priority messages trigger an alert

Testing

We settled on 35° through testing, balancing how clearly the image floats against how compact and stable the device sits on a shelf. We also checked how readable alerts were at a glance from across a room.

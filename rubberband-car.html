Rubber band powered car

A small vehicle driven only by the energy stored in a wound rubber band. No batteries, no motor.

/ The Brief

Build a car powered entirely by a rubber band. All the energy available is whatever you store by winding it before release, so every gram of weight and every bit of friction comes straight out of the distance travelled.

/ Design Priorities
  • Light chassis so more of the stored energy goes into motion
  • Low-friction axles, since smooth rotation matters more than raw torque
  • Wheel to axle ratio, which trades top speed against distance

/ What I Took Away

A fixed energy budget is a useful way to look at most design problems. Every decision either keeps energy, money or time in the system or wastes it, and simple and light usually beats clever and heavy.

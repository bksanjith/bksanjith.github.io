/ The Brief

Design and build a capacitor whose value can be adjusted across a set range, then show that the measured values match what you designed for. Building something that changes capacitance is the easy part. Making it read accurately across the whole range is where most attempts fall short.

/ The Approach

A capacitor's value depends on the overlapping plate area, the gap between the plates and the material between them. I worked out the expected capacitance at points across the range from the parallel-plate relationship, built the device, measured it, and adjusted until the measured values lined up with the calculated ones.

/ What I Took Away

The formula tells you what the value should be. The meter tells you what it is. Most of the work was closing the gap between the two, which is most of engineering in general.

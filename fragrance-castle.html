/ Engineering For The Business

Most of my side projects start here. SwiftPack came from watching how much time went into signing in to seller portals rather than packing. I'm now looking at warehouse automation for our stockroom, which is what took me to CeMAT Asia and PTC Asia in Shanghai in November 2026.

The questions I keep coming back to are not about the technology. They are about total cost, how long labour savings take to pay it back, whether there is local servicing and spare parts, and whether it connects to our existing stock and orders. For a small business, a machine waiting two weeks for a part can cost more than it saves.

/ What This Taught Me
  • Real users don't follow the spec. Customers, suppliers and staff use systems in ways you didn't plan for
  • The bottleneck is rarely where you expect. The slowest part of packing was signing in
  • Cost and payback come first. A solution ten times the price is rarely ten times better

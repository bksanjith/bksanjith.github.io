The problem

Fragrance Castle sells on Shopee, Lazada and Shopify. Each has its own seller portal, its own sign-in and its own way of making a shipping label. Packing an order meant signing in, finding it, downloading the label and sending it to the printer, then doing it all again on the next platform.

Packing was never the slow part. The time went into everything around it. The first label of the day took about 4 minutes, mostly sign-ins, and every label after that took about 30 seconds.

What I built

A small terminal that sits on the packing bench. It goes straight past the sign-in step, pulls pending orders from all three platforms through their APIs, and prints the label with one tap.

  • Hardware: Raspberry Pi, touchscreen and a thermal label printer
  • Software: Python, with requests for the platform APIs and Flask for the interface
  • Interface: modelled on the Bambu Lab X1C printer screen, with big touch targets and very little text, because it gets used in a hurry with full hands

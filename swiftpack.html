PERSONAL PROJECT / FRAGRANCE CASTLE

SwiftPack: an order packing terminal

A Raspberry Pi terminal that brings Shopee, Lazada and Shopify orders onto one touchscreen and prints shipping labels straight to a thermal printer.

/ The Problem

At Fragrance Castle we sell on Shopee, Lazada and Shopify. Each platform has its own seller portal, its own sign-in and its own way of producing a shipping label. Packing an order meant signing in, finding the order, downloading the label and sending it to the printer, then doing it all again on the next platform.

The packing itself was never the slow part. The time went into everything around it. The first label of the day took around 4 minutes, mostly sign-ins, and every label after that took about 30 seconds.

/ What I Built

SwiftPack is a small terminal that sits on the packing bench. It boots straight past the sign-in step, pulls pending orders from all three platforms through their APIs, and prints the label with one tap.

  • Hardware: Raspberry Pi, touchscreen and a thermal label printer
  • Software: Python, using requests for the platform APIs and Flask for the interface
  • Interface: modelled on the Bambu Lab X1C printer screen, with big touch targets and very little text, because it gets used in a hurry with full hands
